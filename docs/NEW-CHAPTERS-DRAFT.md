## 1. Expulsions and "hostility to mankind"

## 3. Herod, the Idumeans and the forced conversion
**Times:** 00:41–01:42
**Summary:** The speaker claims King Herod was an Edomite, and that the group "who say they are Jews and are not" were the Idumeans, forcibly converted by John Hyrcanus around 125 BC during the Hasmonean dynasty after the Maccabean revolt.
